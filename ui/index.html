How is this calculated?

Based on the Zimmerman method (OpenSprinkler): the programmed time is right for a typical summer day (). Each degree colder removes , each % of humidity above the baseline removes , and each mm of rain removes . Rain counts from the two previous days, the run day and the next two days (forecasts count less). The time is never increased. Runs are skipped on frost risk, when it is too cold for grass to grow, or when less than of the time would remain.
